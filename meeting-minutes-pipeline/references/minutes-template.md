# Standalone minutes template

Use this structure for each substantive meeting. Render explanations in output_language; retain these section labels for validation. No companion skill is required.

# Meeting Summary — <grounded topic>

## Date & Time
Source start/end, timezone, provenance, unresolved conflicts. Do not substitute processing time for meeting time.

## Participants
Verified speakers/participants only. Distinguish mentioned people from attendees; uncertain names remain speaker labels.

## Topic
Short content-based title.

## Summary
Discussion points grouped by subject, grounded in source references.

## Root Cause / 原因分析
Include only for troubleshooting. Separate confirmed facts, hypotheses and unknowns; retain exact technical identifiers and supporting examples.

## Action Items
| Due Date | Owner | Action | Acceptance / dependency | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Unknown owners/dates must remain unresolved; do not fabricate commitments. Acceptance criteria inferred by the assistant must be labeled proposals, not meeting decisions.

## Decisions Made
Only actual decisions with supporting source timestamps/utterances/rows. Proposals remain proposals.

## Open Questions
Unresolved questions, blockers and disagreements.

## Source Artifacts
Source type, stable meeting identity, original archive, record/row locator, normalized transcript, genuine provider results if applicable, and demonstrated source limitations.

For post-meeting user corrections, label the update and its source, revise decisions/actions/questions without rewriting original transcript evidence. Do not infer attendance, deadlines, meeting duration or completeness from unsupported metadata. File existence and headings are structural validation only; also review evidence and chunk coverage.
